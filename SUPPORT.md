# Support

## How to get help

1. **Docs first** — [README](README.md), [docs/cli.md](docs/cli.md), [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)
2. **Search issues** — [ROSITO/StreamCraft/issues](https://github.com/ROSITO/StreamCraft/issues)
3. **Open an issue** — use the bug or feature template; include OS, commit, and logs (no cookies / tokens)

## What we cannot help with

- Circumventing DRM or site authentication
- Obtaining copyrighted content without a lawful right to access it
- Debugging proprietary site reverse-engineering

## Community

- Be kind — [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- Security reports — [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
